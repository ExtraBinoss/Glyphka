Glyphka

A generative lettering and symbol studio by Plinka.

Inspiration: Paul Nicholson's geometric constructions and lettering for
Aphex Twin. The associated lettering is known as Xylem. Glyphka uses an
original code-generated alphabet and procedural geometry.

Reference: https://www.monotype.com/resources/special-aphex-twin-logo
Reference: https://www.factmag.com/2017/02/21/aphex-twin-logo-designer-paul-nicholson-shares-blueprints-iconic-design/

Methods:
The alphabet is assembled from original parametric SVG primitives and seeded
variation. Procedural shapes include radial contours, polygons, stars and a
parameterized implementation of Johan Gielis's superformula.
Reference: Johan Gielis (2003), A generic geometric transformation that unifies
a wide range of natural and abstract shapes, American Journal of Botany 90(3).
https://doi.org/10.3732/ajb.90.3.333

SVG morphing uses svgpath normalization and d3-interpolate-path interpolation.
Clipper performs contour unions and differences; Three.js extrudes the contours
in a worker into beveled meshes. Reusable geometry is cached.
